Privacy policy

SCC rclone, a private tool of Stern Consulting & Creative LLC. Effective November 7, 2026.

What it accesses

With the permission of the signed-in Google account, SCC rclone reads files in one Google Drive folder that the company owns (read-only access), and creates files in Google Drive that it uploads itself (transcripts). It does not read Gmail, contacts, calendars or any other Google data.

How the data is used

Recordings are downloaded only to the company's own computer, where they are transcribed. The transcripts are saved back to the company's own Google Drive. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Sharing and storage

No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone. There is no server, no analytics and no tracking: the data stays in the company's Google Drive and on its own computer.

SCC rclone's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

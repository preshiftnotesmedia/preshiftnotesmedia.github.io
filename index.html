SCC rclone

A private tool of Stern Consulting & Creative LLC

SCC rclone is an internal tool, not a public service. It lets the company's own computer copy call recordings out of one Google Drive folder that the company owns, transcribe them on that computer, and save the transcripts back to the company's own Google Drive.

Only the company's own Google account signs in to it. Nobody else's data is read.
